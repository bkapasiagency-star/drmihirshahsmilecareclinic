Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Dr. Mihir Shah's Smile Care Clinic ("the Clinic", "we", "us", or "our"), operating our Dumas Road and Nanpura locations in Surat, Gujarat, is committed to protecting the privacy of our patients and website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your information, in line with the Indian Dental Association's code of ethics on patient confidentiality and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

By using our website or sharing information with us in person, by phone, or through our online enquiry form, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact details you provide through our website enquiry form, phone calls, or WhatsApp, such as your name, phone number, and email address.
  • Appointment and treatment information shared when booking or during a consultation, including preferred treatment, clinic location, and appointment timing.
  • Dental and medical history collected in-clinic as part of diagnosis and treatment, kept strictly in your physical or digital patient record and never published on this website.
  • Technical data such as browser type, device information, and pages visited, collected automatically when you browse our website.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to enquiries and confirm, reschedule, or remind you of appointments.
  • To provide safe, informed dental diagnosis and treatment.
  • To communicate with you via call, SMS, WhatsApp, or email regarding your visit or treatment plan.
  • To maintain patient records as required for continuity of care and applicable medical record-keeping norms.
  • To improve our website and services based on how visitors use them.

We do not use your dental or medical history for marketing purposes, and we never sell your personal information to any third party.

3. Confidentiality of Patient Records

All clinical records, treatment notes, and diagnostic information are treated as strictly confidential. Access is limited to Dr. Mihir Shah and authorised clinical staff directly involved in your care. Patient information is shared with outside parties (such as diagnostic labs, referral specialists, or insurance providers) only with your consent or as required by law.

4. Third-Party Services

Our website uses a small number of third-party services to function, including:

  • Google Maps, to help you locate our clinics.
  • WhatsApp, to enable direct messaging with our front desk for appointment queries.
  • Google Fonts, to load the typography used on this site.

These services may collect limited technical data (such as your IP address) under their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review separately.

5. Cookies

Our website may use basic cookies to remember your preferences and understand how visitors use the site. You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time; this will not affect your ability to contact the clinic or book an appointment.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your personal and health information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse, including restricted access to physical and digital patient files. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security for information sent to us online.

7. Data Retention

We retain patient treatment records for as long as necessary to provide continuing care and to meet applicable medical record-keeping requirements. Website enquiry details are retained only as long as needed to respond to your query or as required by law.

8. Your Rights

  • You may request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • You may request correction of inaccurate contact or personal details.
  • You may request that we stop contacting you for non-clinical (e.g. promotional) communication at any time.
  • You may withdraw consent for sharing your information with third parties, where applicable, subject to our clinical and legal record-keeping obligations.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

9. Children's Privacy

We provide pediatric dental care, which means we may hold health information about minors. Such information is collected only with the consent and involvement of a parent or guardian, and is subject to the same confidentiality standards described in this policy.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when this policy was last revised. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
